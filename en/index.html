04 / ROLEPLAYINGLIFE// there is more
than one path

What’s with the name RolePlayingLife?

I like much the same things in life as I do behind a computer: heading somewhere without a rigid plan, exploring what’s around the corner, trying something new. Life is a little like an RPG — you don’t have to play the same character class forever.

RolePlayingTech is the technology branch of that philosophy. I don’t want to claim expertise in everything. I want to stay curious and have enough tools to take on increasingly interesting problems — and build things properly.

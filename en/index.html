03 / ABOUT

How I ended up here

Before IT, I worked in print shops, retail and warehouses, among other places. Then came software testing, ERP implementations, DevOps and programming. I eventually became a senior developer, but the work gradually stopped being enjoyable. I left. AI got me tinkering with technology again, this time on my own terms.

RolePlayingLife is how I think about life. A trip with a tent, an unfamiliar route, a new project — I like learning something along the way.

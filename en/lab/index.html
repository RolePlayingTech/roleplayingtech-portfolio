ISSUE 04 · NOVEMBER 2026 ENERGY SYSTEMS · PSE GRID TELEMETRY (2024–2026)

Shutting Off Cheap Solar When Power Is Expensive: Inside 73,924 Polish Grid Intervals

A continuous 15-minute telemetry audit across six PSE API streams (Sep 24, 2024 – Nov 2, 2026), unit-level generation for 106 dispatchable units across 37 power stations (gen-jw), and all 6 cross-border interconnectors. Shows that 66.57% of Poland's 2026 renewable curtailment (1,201.57 of 1,805.05 GWh) occurred at positive wholesale prices and 27.56% (497.42 GWh) at high prices RCE ≥ 250 PLN/MWh — driven by a ~7.98 GW midday coal and gas boiler floor held online to survive the +5.64 GW climb to 13.63 GW at 20:00, while 96.75% of large curtailment jumps (≥ 200 MW) strike rigidly at minute :00 on the full clock hour.

PLN 1.48B for “Cybersecure Local Gov”, yet 1 in 4 municipal email domains remain spoofable

ISSUE 02 · NOVEMBER 2026 PUBLIC REGISTRIES · CYBERSECURITY

How Polish State Registries and .gov.pl Portals Funnel Traffic to Unlicensed Casinos

A cross-registry audit of 35,583 unique school, hospital and nursery domains from RSPO, RPWDL2 and the National Nursery Register matched against 59,376 entries in the Ministry of Finance's illegal gambling blocklist. Documents 291 active, unblocked domains routing Polish visitors to unlicensed casinos (287 unblocked end-to-end), 158 still displayed on live official institution cards (plus 137 in the Ministry of Digital Affairs open dataset), 9 official .gov.pl, county and BIP pages carrying 10 live dofollow anchors, and a 26.2× surge in .pl root domains inside the MF blocklist (from 0.82% in 2023 to 21.51% in 2026). Separates three technical vectors — including 8 active public school and hospital servers with injected PHP spam where MF blacklisting would trigger mandatory 48h domain-wide ISP blocking under Art. 15f(5).

02 / Method

Why pair reporting with code

Generic commentary is easy to generate and just as easy to scroll past. Audited public records and tools that let readers inspect their own city, hospital or sector hold up much better.

01 · Source trail

Every figure links to a filing

Every row in the datasets published here carries an official proceeding ID and direct URL to the public registry, so journalists, researchers and readers can verify amounts and vendors in seconds.

02 · Code + manual audit

Catching dirty registry bugs

Scripts handle API harvesting, deduplication and cross-section checks, while edge cases, clerical trailing zeros and misleading department names are reviewed manually before publication.

03 · Newsroom-friendly

Open data & clean embeds

Cleaned JSON and CSV exports are free to download, and interactive explorers include a lightweight ?embed=1 mode so newsrooms can embed them in articles with source attribution.

03 / Editorial & research collaboration

Have a messy dataset or a story idea worth building out?

I collaborate with editors, journalists and research teams on data investigations, interactive article calculators and custom pipelines for auditing public registries or document dumps. A few sentences by email are plenty to start.

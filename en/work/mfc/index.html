Electronics and software for an actual research experiment.

01 / ORIGIN

Where it started

The experiment required measurements from a microbial fuel cell and control over parts of the setup. A ready-made solution wasn't quite what the research needed.

02 / THE WORK

What I did

I put together the electronics, connected sensors and microcontrollers, and built a data collection system and web dashboard. AI helped, especially where I had to learn more about an unfamiliar area.

Where it's at

The station was used during PhD research. That phase of the experiment has finished, but we plan to develop the system further.

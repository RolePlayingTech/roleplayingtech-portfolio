MFC Monitoring Station

A request for help with research turned into a measurement station with electronics, data logging and remote controls.

01 / Where it started

Microbial fuel cell research called for a setup that could log measurements and control parts of an experiment.

02 / What came out of it

I assembled a station with microcontrollers and measurement electronics, connected it to the internet and built a monitoring and control dashboard.

03 / My part

I handled engineering — hardware, integration and software, also using AI during development. The research itself is conducted by a PhD researcher.

04 / Where it stands now

The station was used during research. That measurement series has ended, with more development planned.

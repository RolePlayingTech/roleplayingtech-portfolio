MFC Monitoring Station

A measurement and control station for microbial fuel cell research.

monitor-mfc.roleplayingtech.com ↗

01—03

Where it started

A PhD researcher at Cracow University of Technology, working on microbial fuel cells, needed a way to collect measurements and control parts of the setup remotely. It meant bringing environmental engineering, electronics and software together in one working rig.

What I worked on

I built a setup with microcontrollers, sensors and controls, plus software to collect and display data in a browser. I handled the technical side, not the underlying research. Much of it was new to me — AI helped me catch up quickly on what I didn't know.

Where it's at

The station was used in an experiment. That series of measurements has ended, and further work on the system is possible.

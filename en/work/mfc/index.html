MFC Monitoring Station

A measurement and control station for microbial fuel cell research.

RPT / mfc

01—03

Where it started

A friend working on PhD research needed a way to collect measurements and control parts of an experiment remotely.

What I worked on

I built a setup with microcontrollers, sensors and controls, plus software to collect and display data in a browser. I handled the technical side, not the underlying research.

Where it's at

The station was used in an experiment. That series of measurements has ended, and further work on the system is possible.

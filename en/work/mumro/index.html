Mumro

A publishing tool that grew into a much bigger application.

RPT / mumro

Where it started

While working on Świadek Dziejów, I kept spending time on the same content and publishing tasks. I wanted to stop doing them manually.

What I worked on

I started building tools for content and automation, then connected them into Mumro. I work on the architecture, decide what to build, test and improve it. AI agents help extensively with implementation.

Where it's at

I use Mumro for my own projects and keep developing it. I may open it up to selected partners, but it's not a public subscription SaaS yet.

Mumro

It started as a tool for publishing videos. It has grown into a substantial system for content, AI and automation.

Mumro
01 / Where it started

As I ran Świadek Dziejów, more and more time went into planning, publishing and managing content. I didn’t want to repeat the same work by hand.

02 / What came out of it

Some simple tools gradually grew into Mumro: a platform connecting content creation, scheduling, integrations and automation.

03 / My part

I build it independently. I design features and architecture, direct AI models during implementation, test, refine and consider security and performance.

04 / Where it stands now

For now, I mainly use Mumro for my own projects; selected partner collaborations and broader commercialization may come later.

Świadek Dziejów

It was supposed to be an experiment with generative video. It gradually became a full history and education project.

Świadek Dziejów
01 / Where it started

When Veo 3 arrived, I started exploring historical storytelling through generative video. I didn’t want the work to end at novelty or become low-value content.

02 / What came out of it

A recognizable character, films, educational graphics, articles, a website with Polish history and an atlas, plus audiences across multiple platforms.

03 / My part

I experiment with formats, models and storytelling, produce media and develop the wider ecosystem. Mumro helps me plan and promote content.

04 / Where it stands now

The project is active and evolving, with over 50K combined follows across TikTok, Facebook, Instagram and YouTube; educators have also shown interest.

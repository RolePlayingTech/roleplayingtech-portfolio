Crewly

I know this from sailing: finding a crew through scattered Facebook groups is far from ideal. That’s why Crewly exists.

Crewly
01 / Where it started

Sailors and skippers search across fragmented groups, posts and messages. It’s not a particularly useful way to connect people.

02 / What came out of it

Together we created a platform meant to make it easier to find crews and opportunities to sail.

03 / My part

I co-create the project; it isn’t presented as a solo build.

04 / Where it stands now

Crewly is live and attracting its first customers, though still at an early stage.

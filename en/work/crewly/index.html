Crewly

A place to find sailing crews, skippers and trips.

crewly.pl ↗

01—03

Where it started

I don't sail myself — my brother does. He knows the sailing world from the inside and saw how awkward it is to look for a crew or a skipper across dozens of scattered Facebook groups.

What I worked on

Together we're building Crewly — a place where it's easier to list a trip, put a crew together or join someone on board. I handle the product and technical side.

Where it's at

The platform is live with early users and customers. We're still developing it.
